# LONGKAKA SELLER AI — Brand and Icon Specification

## Approved app icon

The project owner approved the generated square icon on 2026-11-02 for LONGKAKA SELLER AI.

The approved artwork is the glossy deep-blue/neon-cyan AI video clapperboard, with a gold motion accent, AI chip, Chinese-to-Vietnamese translation bubbles, and LONGKAKA / SELLER AI wordmark.

## Brand system

- Primary background: deep navy / midnight blue.
- Primary accent: electric blue and cyan glow.
- Secondary accent: warm gold for actions, highlights, and sales outcomes.
- Text: high-contrast white with restrained secondary gray.
- Visual language: polished, modern, premium AI-video studio; avoid clutter and excessive glow in working screens.

## App UI direction

- Android launcher icon: use the approved artwork, with adaptive-icon foreground/background assets generated from the source artwork when the Android app module is created.
- Main screen: one-screen workflow, dark blue technology theme.
- Primary action: clear cyan/blue action button; gold reserved for important sales/export emphasis.
- Keep the icon's detailed wordmark artwork for store/splash/brand surfaces. For the small launcher glyph, prepare a simplified crop/foreground so it remains legible at Android icon sizes.

## Asset handling

The approved source image is 1254 × 1254 PNG. Store the original as a project design asset and derive Android density/adaptive-icon variants from it during app implementation. Do not redraw or replace the approved artwork without the owner's request.
